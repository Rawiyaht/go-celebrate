GoCelebrate Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

GoCelebrate collects information you provide directly, including event details, guest names, RSVP status, and contact information entered when planning events.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to power the GoCelebrate app features including event planning, guest list management, and RSVP tracking.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties.

4. Cookies

GoCelebrate does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies of any kind.

5. Children's Privacy

GoCelebrate is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@gocelebrate.app
